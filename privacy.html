DirtScan

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

DirtScan doesn’t collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you enter and scan stays on your device.

Who we are

DirtScan: Concrete Calculator (“DirtScan”) is published on the App Store by QIDIENT GLOBAL LTD (“we”). This policy covers the DirtScan app for iPhone and iPad.

What stays on your device

Your jobs and projects, calculator inputs and results, scans, tape checks, job and project photos, quotes, order sheets, pour outcomes, your company profile (logo, company name, phone, email and address), your answers to the welcome questions (including your first name, if you give it) and your app settings are stored only on your device. DirtScan doesn’t connect to any server of ours, and we never receive this data.

Deleting DirtScan deletes this data. Your device’s own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include it, under the settings you chose for those backups.

Camera and LiDAR scanning

On an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR, a scan uses the camera and the LiDAR sensor to measure the ground in front of you. The readings are turned into a height map of the ground on your device. DirtScan keeps that height map and the measurements made from it with the job, so you can reopen the result. Camera pictures from a scan aren’t kept or sent anywhere.

Photos

You can add photos to a job or project with the camera or the system photo picker, and a company logo with the camera or the photo picker. The photo picker gives DirtScan only the images you choose; DirtScan doesn’t access the rest of your library. DirtScan keeps a copy of each image on your device, with location and other metadata removed. Photos you take in DirtScan aren’t added to your photo library. Nothing is uploaded.

Location

DirtScan doesn’t use Location Services. Addresses you type for a project, an order sheet or your company stay on your device with the rest of your data.

Documents and sharing

Job reports, order sheets, quotes and share text are created on your device from your jobs. They leave your device only when you share them yourself, through the share sheet, to a place you choose, such as a message to your supplier or client. DirtScan keeps a list of what you shared and when, on your device, so it can show a project’s documents.

Subscriptions

Purchases and free trials are handled by Apple through the App Store. DirtScan receives your subscription status from the App Store so it can unlock DirtScan Pro. We don’t receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the App Store.

Ratings

DirtScan may use Apple’s standard prompt to ask whether you’d like to rate the app. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

DirtScan contains no analytics, advertising or attribution software and doesn’t track you across apps or websites. If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your device’s Analytics & Improvements settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us. Apple controls that sharing.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to reply to you.

Children

DirtScan is a tool for contractors and homeowners planning concrete and excavation work and isn’t directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version ships with an app update and shows a new effective date here. For questions, see the DirtScan support page.
