Support

DirtScan: Concrete Calculator works out the yards of concrete, dirt and gravel to order, for iPhone and iPad. It is published by QIDIENT GLOBAL LTD.

Contact us

Questions, problems or feedback — email us at support@qidientlabs.com.

To help us reply faster, tell us:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version,
  • what you were doing when the problem happened, and
  • a screenshot, if it helps explain.

Subscriptions and billing

DirtScan Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. To see or cancel your subscription, open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

For refund requests, contact Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

DirtScan doesn’t collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
